## Smart Municipality

A team project that brings a municipality's services onto an OpenStreetMap map, so residents can find offices, facilities and services near them. The map supports updates and a satellite view.

## Blood Map

Connects patients who need blood with possible donors nearby.

- A map with markers for patients who need a specific blood group
- Donors register and show when they are available to give blood
- Search by blood group or location
- Donors near a new request are notified

## Route mapping

Interactive maps that draw routes between places, used for local planning and the municipality project.
